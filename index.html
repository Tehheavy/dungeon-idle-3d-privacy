Dungeon Idle 3D – Privacy Policy

Developer: Salmon Gamer · Effective date: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Dungeon Idle 3D (the "game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The game is published on Google Play by Salmon Gamer ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or other personal details.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage data to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases (such as Remove Ads or premium heroes) are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress, settings and the list of items you bought are stored locally on your device. We do not upload them to any server and cannot see them. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them; purchases can be restored from Google Play.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players who haven't bought Remove Ads can watch optional rewarded ads. These are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device or other identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location, derived from your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics and crash logs

This data is sent over an encrypted connection. Google's use of it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options in the game. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The game only receives confirmation of which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them after a reinstall. Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not share it with anyone other than Google, as described above for ads and purchases.

Your choices

  • Buying Remove Ads stops all ads and ad-related data collection by the game.
  • You can change ad consent from Settings → Ad privacy options (where available) and manage your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all data it stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
